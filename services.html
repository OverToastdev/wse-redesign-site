Start a project

From the first sketch to a running machine on your floor: everything is handled by one team in our Ipoh workshop.

If a step on your line eats labour, we study it, then design the simplest machine that removes it. Design, fabrication, wiring, installation: all under one roof.

1

Custom automation machines

Our core work. Tell us the manual task slowing your line down: filling, packing, lifting, sorting, feeding. We design a machine around your exact process, product and floor space. Typical builds include filling and packing machines, lifting and handling systems, feeding and sorting equipment, and process-specific one-offs.

2

Conveyor systems

Belt conveyors, roller conveyors and vacuum-board transport, engineered to your layout, load and speed. From a single transfer section to a connected production line, including frames, drives and controls.

3

Machine retrofit & automation upgrades

You do not always need a new machine. We add automation to equipment you already own: powered feeds, sensors, pneumatic actuators, controls. Cutting the manual steps out of machines that otherwise work fine.

4

Metal fabrication & machining

In-house fabrication keeps quality and lead time under our control: machine frames, stainless hoppers and chutes, safety guards, platforms, brackets and replacement parts. Built to drawing or to sample.

5

Installation, commissioning & support

The team that builds your machine delivers and installs it. We commission on-site, train your operators, and stay a phone call away for servicing, adjustments and spare parts.

6

Free feasibility discussion

Not sure your process can be automated? Send photos or a short video of the task. We give you a straight answer: what can be automated, roughly what it takes, and whether it is worth doing.

Not sure where to start?

Start with the task, not the machine. Send us a video and we will tell you honestly if it is worth automating.
