Services

Engineering services, end to end

From the first sketch to a running machine on your factory floor — everything is handled by one team in our Ipoh workshop.

Custom Automation Machine Design & Build

Our core work. Tell us the manual task slowing your line down — filling, packing, lifting, sorting, feeding — and we design a machine around your exact process, product and floor space. Typical applications:

Filling & packing machines · lifting & handling systems · feeding & sorting equipment · process-specific one-off machines.

Conveyor Systems

Belt conveyors, roller conveyors and vacuum-board transport — engineered to your layout, load and speed requirements. From a single transfer section to a connected production line, including frames, drives and controls.

Machine Retrofit & Automation Upgrades

You don't always need a new machine. We add automation to equipment you already own — powered feeds, sensors, pneumatic actuators, controls — cutting the manual steps out of machines that otherwise work fine.

Metal Fabrication & Machining

In-house fabrication keeps quality and lead time under our control: machine frames, stainless hoppers and chutes, safety guards, platforms, brackets and replacement parts — built to drawing or to sample.

Installation, Commissioning & Support

The team that builds your machine delivers and installs it. We commission on-site, train your operators, and remain a phone call away for servicing, adjustments and spare parts.

Free Feasibility Discussion

Not sure if your process can be automated? Send us photos or a short video of the task. We'll give you a straight answer — what can be automated, roughly what it takes, and whether it's worth doing.

How we work

From problem to running machine

  1. Understand the process

    We study the task on your floor (or from your videos/photos) — cycle time, product, space, and what's actually costing you labour.

  2. Design & proposal

    Our designer proposes a machine concept with drawings, scope and a fixed quotation. No surprises later.

  3. Build in-house

    Fabrication, machining, assembly and wiring happen in our Ipoh workshop. You're welcome to visit and see progress.

  4. Test & commission

    We test the machine before delivery, then install and commission it on your floor with your product.

  5. After-sales support

    Operator training, adjustments and servicing — from the same people who designed and built it.
