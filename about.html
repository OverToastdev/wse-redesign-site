We like solving problems, and making ideas come to life.

Weng Sun Engineering Works Sdn Bhd builds custom machines in Ipoh, Perak: incorporated 1993, automating Malaysian production lines ever since.

Factories come to us with the same kind of problem: a step in production that eats manpower, slows the line, or strains workers. Our answer is always the same. A machine designed for exactly that task. Not a catalogue product adapted to fit, but equipment engineered around your process, product and floor space.

Being a single workshop means the person who designs your machine is the person who builds it, installs it and answers the phone afterwards.

The designer

Kenny Cheong, founder and chief designer: a mechanical engineer who has spent his career on factory floors, watching how work actually gets done, then designing machines that do it better. He leads every project from concept to commissioning.

Why it matters

Most automation suppliers sell standard machines and leave you to adapt. We do the opposite: we study your process first, then build the machine to match. With one designer-owner overseeing everything, nothing gets lost between sales, engineering and fabrication.
