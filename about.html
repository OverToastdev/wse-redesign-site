About us

We like solving problems — and making ideas come to life

Weng Sun Engineering Works Sdn Bhd is an automation and custom machine builder based in Ipoh, Perak, serving Malaysian manufacturers for over 20 years.

Our story

A workshop built on practical engineering

For more than two decades, factories across Malaysia have come to us with the same kind of problem: a step in their production that eats up manpower, slows the line, or strains their workers.

Our answer has always been the same — a machine designed for exactly that task. Not a catalogue product adapted to fit, but equipment engineered around the customer's process, product and floor space. That focus has taken our machines into rubber product factories, ice plants, transport operations, feed mills and stone processors.

Being a single workshop means the person who designs your machine is the person who builds it, installs it and answers the phone afterwards. That accountability is what our customers value most.

The designer

Kenny Cheong — Founder & Chief Designer

A mechanical engineering specialist with over 15 years of hands-on experience, Kenny has spent his career on factory floors — watching how work actually gets done, then designing machines that do it better.

  • 15+ years in mechanical engineering and machine design
  • Specialises in converting labour-intensive actions into automation
  • Personally leads every project from concept to commissioning
  • Designs for simplicity — machines your own team can run and maintain

Why that matters to you

Most automation suppliers sell standard machines and leave you to adapt. We do the opposite: we study your process first, then build the machine to match. With one designer-owner overseeing everything, nothing gets lost between sales, engineering and fabrication.

The result: equipment that fits your line the day it's installed — and a builder who stays accountable long after.
