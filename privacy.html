Personal Data Protection Notice (English)

Issued in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 ("PDPA") of Malaysia. Last updated: November 2026.

1. Who we are

Weng Sun Engineering Works Sdn Bhd ("we", "us", "our"), company registration no. 199301012690 (267428-M), is a mechanical engineering company based at No 8 & 10, Laluan Kangsar 8, Taman Perindustrian Saujana, Off Jalan Kuala Kangsar, 30010 Ipoh, Perak, Malaysia.

2. Personal data we collect

When you contact us through this website, WhatsApp, telephone or email, we may collect:

  • Your name and company name
  • Your telephone number and/or email address
  • Details of your enquiry, project requirements, and any photos, videos or documents you choose to send us

This website does not use tracking cookies, analytics cookies or advertising cookies.

3. Purpose of collection

Your personal data is collected and used solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and communicate with you
  • Prepare quotations, proposals and machine designs
  • Deliver, install and service equipment you order from us
  • Meet legal, accounting and record-keeping obligations

4. Disclosure

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. Your data may be disclosed only to our employees and contractors who need it to serve you, and to authorities where required by Malaysian law.

5. Retention and security

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above, and take reasonable practical steps to protect it from loss, misuse and unauthorised access.

6. Your rights

Under the PDPA you may request access to your personal data, request correction of inaccurate data, or withdraw your consent to our processing of it (subject to legal or contractual restrictions). To exercise these rights, contact us at +605 506 1811 or +6012 457 9130.

7. Consent

By submitting an enquiry through this website or by contacting us via WhatsApp, telephone or email, you consent to the collection and use of your personal data as described in this Notice. WhatsApp is operated by Meta Platforms, Inc.; its own privacy policy applies to messages sent through it.

Dikeluarkan menurut Akta Perlindungan Data Peribadi 2010 ("PDPA") Malaysia. Dikemas kini: November 2026.

1. Siapa kami

Weng Sun Engineering Works Sdn Bhd ("kami"), nombor pendaftaran syarikat 199301012690 (267428-M), ialah sebuah syarikat kejuruteraan mekanikal yang beralamat di No 8 & 10, Laluan Kangsar 8, Taman Perindustrian Saujana, Off Jalan Kuala Kangsar, 30010 Ipoh, Perak, Malaysia.

2. Data peribadi yang kami kumpul

Apabila anda menghubungi kami melalui laman web ini, WhatsApp, telefon atau e-mel, kami mungkin mengumpul:

  • Nama anda dan nama syarikat anda
  • Nombor telefon dan/atau alamat e-mel anda
  • Butiran pertanyaan anda, keperluan projek, dan sebarang foto, video atau dokumen yang anda hantarkan

Laman web ini tidak menggunakan kuki penjejakan, kuki analitik atau kuki pengiklanan.

3. Tujuan pengumpulan

Data peribadi anda dikumpul dan digunakan semata-mata untuk:

  • Menjawab pertanyaan anda dan berkomunikasi dengan anda
  • Menyediakan sebut harga, cadangan dan reka bentuk mesin
  • Menghantar, memasang dan menyelenggara peralatan yang anda pesan
  • Memenuhi kewajipan undang-undang, perakaunan dan penyimpanan rekod

4. Pendedahan

Kami tidak menjual, menyewa atau memperdagangkan data peribadi anda. Data anda hanya boleh didedahkan kepada pekerja dan kontraktor kami yang memerlukannya, dan kepada pihak berkuasa sebagaimana yang dikehendaki oleh undang-undang Malaysia.

Kami menyimpan data peribadi hanya selama yang diperlukan, dan mengambil langkah praktikal yang munasabah untuk melindunginya daripada kehilangan, penyalahgunaan dan akses tanpa kebenaran.
